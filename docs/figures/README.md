# Figure captions and provenance

All three figures are generated by [../make_figures.py](../make_figures.py).
Run `.venv/bin/python docs/make_figures.py` from the repository root after
installing `./tool[plot]`. Outputs are 300 dpi PNGs and SVGs with editable text.
The script uses Matplotlib's bundled DejaVu Sans font, the colour-blind safe Okabe–Ito
blue/orange pair, and the perceptually uniform cividis probability scale.
It reads committed data only, runs one process, closes each figure before
creating the next, and does not fit or regenerate targets.

## Headline: noise families and resolved residuals

[PNG](headline.png) · [SVG](headline.svg)

Finite independent-source local models for the Renou family at `u² = 0.85`
and the elegant joint measurement (EJM), under independent source noise and
independent detector noise. Top panels show Euclidean model-to-target
distances; bottom panels resolve small positive distances on logarithmic axes.
Markers are selected certified candidates from
[best-fits.csv](../../benchmarks/best-fits.csv), column `L2_rational`.
Connecting lines guide the eye and do not certify intermediate parameters.
Exact zeros are omitted on log axes; they are not replaced by a plotting floor.

Dashed lines connect `published_reference_L2` values from the same CSV.
These are evaluations of the approximate 2020 Eq. (4) estimate:
`d_est(v) = 0` for `v <= v*`, and
`d_est(v) = ||P(v) - P(v*)||₂ sin(theta)` for `v > v*`.
The approximate transition/angle pairs are EJM source `(0.80, 50°)`,
EJM detector `(0.86, 60°)`, Renou source `(0.89, 6°)`, and Renou detector
`(0.91, 6°)`. Dotted lines show these estimated transitions. The reference
values are approximate analytic estimates, not digitized neural-network data
and not rigorous lower bounds.

Each plotted model is local, so its certified distance is an upper bound on
distance to the unrestricted local set. Positive residuals do not establish
nonlocality; small residuals do not prove exact target locality. The comparisons
do not certify a new locality threshold or claim to improve on the authors'
best saved model. Renou certificates refer to stored rational approximations
of quantum targets. The EJM noise targets are exact rational polynomials.

Reference: Tamás Kriváchy et al., *npj Quantum Information* **6**, 70 (2020),
[doi:10.1038/s41534-020-00305-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-020-00305-x),
Eqs. (3)–(4) and Figs. 4–5. See [RESULTS.md](../../RESULTS.md) and
[LITERATURE.md](../../LITERATURE.md) for reference and target checks.

## A saved local model

[PNG](local-model.png) · [SVG](local-model.svg)

The actual rational model in
[117-renou-detector-93-100-hybrid-reverse.json.gz](../../benchmarks/models/117-renou-detector-93-100-hybrid-reverse.json.gz),
for Renou detector noise at `u² = 0.85`, `v = 0.93`. Its source alphabets
each contain 12 stored symbols, with respectively 8, 9, and 2 positive-weight
symbols for alpha, beta, and gamma. Bars show each source law separately;
their product is the joint hidden-variable law. The three heatmap rows show
`P_A(a | beta, gamma)`, `P_B(b | gamma, alpha)`, and
`P_C(c | alpha, beta)`, for outputs 0–3. Each response row sums exactly to one.
Colours show floating-point representations of the stored rational values;
responses are not rounded to a winning output. Pale grey marks cells that
never contribute because an incident source symbol has zero weight. All axes
retain the original, zero-based symbol indices.

The certified Euclidean distance is `8.207624541e-9`, and L1 is
`3.848594064e-8` (display decimals). Its status is `nearby_local_model`,
with `exact_match: false` and L1 tolerance `1/1000000`. It certifies a nearby
local distribution rather than exact equality to the stored target.

## Round comparison and selected ablation

[PNG](search-improvement.png) · [SVG](search-improvement.svg)

Left: round-1 and round-2 certified Euclidean distances at the four reported
2020 noise transitions, using
[round1/best-fits.csv](../../benchmarks/round1/best-fits.csv) and
[best-fits.csv](../../benchmarks/best-fits.csv).
Right: six selected EJM source-noise component trials at `v = 0.80` from
[ablation.csv](../../benchmarks/ablation.csv), illustrating alphabet expansion,
seeding, fixed/free-weight convex polishing, and the seeded hybrid with
perturbation searches. Both polishing variants start from the same seeded
L-BFGS candidate. The hybrid is a separate search from that seed, not a final
step after the free-weight polishing trial. The other component trials remain
in the CSV and in [RESULTS.md](../../RESULTS.md).

Bar origins are `1e-11` on a logarithmic axis; labels give the actual distances.
Trials use different alphabet sizes, restart counts, and iteration caps.
These are descriptive comparisons, not equal-budget statistical or hardware
speed comparisons. The ablation's best displayed distance `5.063783258e-10`
is distinct from the final sweep's selected `5.773336608e-11` at the same target.

## Checks

Before plotting, the script binds all 52 target-summary rows and 8 control rows
to their 50 selected bundles, checks model/target hashes, recomputes distances from the
saved rational output probabilities, and compares displayed CSV distances.
It also checks every exact source and response simplex in the visualized model.
These checks complement the independent full-model contraction audit in
[../../benchmarks/independent_check.py](../../benchmarks/independent_check.py).
Round-1 and ablation figures use archived CSV measurements; their intermediate
models are not required for regeneration.
